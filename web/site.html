Kinetica

A lightweight 2D physics engine for games, written in Rust. Everything below runs in your browser.

Try the engine

Testbed 45 scenes, one idea each: body types, joints, collisions, queries. Grab, launch and blast things.

Play the games

SlingshotKnock down towers of wood, glass and stone. TrialsDrive a little car over hills, a bridge and a jump. PlatformerRun and jump across moving platforms, push a box. SnipCut the ropes to drop the sweet in the basket. FruitsDrop fruits in a jar; two alike merge into a bigger one.
